Open compliance items
---------------------
The LGPL libraries libewf, libfsapfs and libfsrefs are currently linked
statically and the release archive does not yet provide object files, a relink
recipe, or a corresponding source archive. Until one of those is provided, the
LGPL-3.0 relinking requirement is not satisfied. Track this in docs/LICENSES.md
before publishing a binary release that enables those backends.

No third-party component is required when the optional backends are disabled at
configure time (-DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBTSK=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBEWF=OFF
-DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBWIM=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBFSAPFS=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBFSREFS=OFF);
only zlib remains, and it is required for DMG/GHO decompression.
