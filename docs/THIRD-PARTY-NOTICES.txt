Builds without optional backends
--------------------------------
No third-party component other than zlib is required when the optional backends
are disabled at configure time (-DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBTSK=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBEWF=OFF
-DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBWIM=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBFSAPFS=OFF -DUIFE_ENABLE_LIBFSREFS=OFF);
zlib stays mandatory because DMG/GHO decompression depends on it.
